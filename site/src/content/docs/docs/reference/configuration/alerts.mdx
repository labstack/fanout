## Notes

### `FANOUT_ALERTS_EMAIL_DEFAULT_TO`

A comma-separated list of addresses that receive alerts from rules with no webhook and no email recipients of their own; requires SMTP.

### `FANOUT_ALERTS_EMAIL_HOURLY_LIMIT`

Caps the alert emails sent to one address in any rolling hour, across all rules. Notifications over the limit are suppressed, not delayed, and shown on the rule; 0 suppresses all alert email.

### `FANOUT_ALERTS_ENABLED`

Runs the alert evaluation engine.

### `FANOUT_ALERTS_EVALUATION_INTERVAL`

Schedules alert evaluation; use a Go duration of at least 1s.

### `FANOUT_ALERTS_HISTORY_DAYS`

Sets alert history retention in days.
